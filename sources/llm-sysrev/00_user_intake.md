## Research Topic

**Large language models as co-authors in systematic literature reviews: accuracy, bias, and reproducibility**

Core question: To what extent can LLMs reliably perform the screening, data extraction, and synthesis steps of a systematic review without introducing factual errors, hallucinated citations, or selection bias — and how does performance compare to human reviewers?

## Anti-AI-Watermark

**Active.** The academic-writer must apply:
- Irregular sentence rhythm and paragraph length variation
- Domain-specific lexical grounding from retrieved literature (not generic academic filler)
- Counterpoints and hedges placed where a human scholar would insert doubt
- Passive voice only where disciplinary convention demands it
- No repeated connective patterns; transitions emerge from logical structure
